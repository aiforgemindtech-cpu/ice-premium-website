Most persistent roof leaks are not failures of the covering. They are failures at the junctions: parapets, upstands, penetrations, abutments. That is why patching the tile or the sheet never fixes them for long. We find where the water is actually getting in before we quote a repair.

## Diagnosis before re-covering

A leak that has been patched several times without success is telling you the diagnosis was wrong, not that the patch was poor. Water tracks: it can enter at a parapet and appear metres away. Before we price a re-cover we establish the entry point, because re-covering a roof that leaks at the upstand simply produces a new roof that leaks at the upstand.

## The structure under the covering

When we strip a covering we inspect the structure beneath it. Prolonged ingress decays timber purlins and noggins, and laying a new covering over a compromised structure is both unsafe and short-lived. Structural repairs are identified at strip-out and confirmed with you in writing before the new covering goes on.

## Rainwater goods

Gutters, downpipes and their outfalls are part of the waterproofing system, not an accessory to it. Undersized or blocked rainwater goods cause overflow that saturates walls and undermines everything above. We size and install them as part of the roof scope rather than treating them separately.
